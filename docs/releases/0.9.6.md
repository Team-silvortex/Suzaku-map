## Three bounded candidate-compatibility repairs

This release includes **production candidate-routing and page-policy changes**
from the entryway work; it is not solely a data update:

1. **Chinese adopted-syllable continuation:** an existing Word's aligned,
   complete Pinyin syllable can witness a previously missing authored
   continuation after already adopted Han text. The reading must have a real
   finished decoder path; Prefix-only guesses cannot authorize a new Han
   reading. The original written prefix and typing anchor remain, existing
   reachable authors keep their pages, and only actual local sentence
   identities qualify for the new reservation.
2. **Chinese terminal `ng`:** a uniquely sourced whole Word can retain its own
   authored pair when the unfinished reading is missing exactly the final `g`.
   Han/syllable alignment and original finished-path provenance must agree;
   this is not inference from a re-sorted candidate page or general recovery
   of unrelated ambiguous branches.
3. **Japanese short nasal parent:** at the default twelve-row capacity only,
   a unique shorter Word missing its final `ん` can retain its own pair when a
   real longer compound of the same reading/written family competes. The
   published Word positions, phonetic anchors and actual local author rows
   must provide the witnesses, including literal Kana forms. Other capacity
   policies keep their existing behavior.

The decoder's existing paths are not rewritten. These narrow rules do not add
arbitrary homophone substitution, contextual polyphone inference, Japanese
morphology or unlimited prediction. They preserve the tested typing anchors,
relative Word order, scores and literal rescue in their bounded repair paths;
new dictionary entries can still change short-prefix pools, index-derived
scores and pages. Preserving old source data is not a guarantee that every
old short-root generated choice remains visible.

## Preserved contracts and regression scope

All **25 recommended data-only packs** (eight English, eight Chinese and nine
Japanese) and the pack SDK remain unchanged. The model/provider protocol,
startup catalog policy, sixteen installed-pack slots, source/pack work budgets,
**six-row page size and default twelve-row candidate list** are not expanded.
No model becomes a hard dependency or is automatically downloaded.

Regression suites exercise complete and selected partial spellings, case,
Pinyin tones, fixed Kana oracles, six horizontal gaps, explicit adoption and
long-prefix preservation, seven line boundaries, opaque tokens, raw rescue,
single explicit commit and undo. Private child processes exercise optional
pack coexistence without using the personal registry. Synthetic disabled,
pending, missing, failed, timed-out and empty providers check local-pool
availability, not real-model quality. The table-item suite introduces a
test-only shared helper without migrating the older suites. Historical
washroom/pocket author windows are bounded independently of current totals;
old raw-resource hashes and reviewed golden baselines are not refreshed.
