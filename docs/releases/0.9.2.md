# 0.9.2 Everyday fallback and measured local-input work

Suzaku Map 0.9.2 prepares three finite vocabulary expansions for messages,
appointments/walk-ins and aftercare/packaging, plus narrowly scoped English
and Japanese candidate-computation optimizations. It remains a **Linux / IBus
Alpha**, targeting **Ubuntu 24.04 amd64, X11 or GNOME/XWayland**. Other
platforms' synchronized version numbers do not establish native input acceptance.

**Preparation status:** this document is not a publication or package-acceptance
receipt. Local validation of the versioned **0.9.2 / build 42** tree passes
2,313 Rust checks, the separate 19 private UI gates and strict Clippy.
Its exact commit still needs remote CI and accepted-package validation
before a tag and downloadable Linux prerelease can be published. Earlier scoped
checks and the published 0.9.1 assets do not accept this new version.

## Everyday offline fallback

Compared with **0.9.1**, the three built-in data batches add:

- English: **22 contexts and 44 sentences**, with no new indexed forms or
  change to their first-occurrence order.
- Simplified Chinese: **66 Word readings, 22 contexts and 44 authored sentences**.
- Japanese: **34 Word readings, 17 contexts and 34 continuations**.

This is **100 CJK readings and 122 trilingual authored sentences/continuations**.
Totals are **6,143 English indexed forms / 795 contexts / 1,554 sentences**
(43 ordered layers), **3,244 Chinese readings / 789 contexts / 1,576 sentences**,
and **556 Japanese readings / 279 contexts / 557 continuation records**.
These are finite authored records, not language-quality scores, open-ended
generation or a complete Chinese/Japanese dictionary.

- Messages: missed-message, later-reply, arrangement-confirmation and feedback
  examples such as `i only just saw your messag` and `刚看到你的消息`, with
  their explicit words and authored continuations.
- Appointments and queues: `do i need to book ahead`, `qu'hao` → `取号`, and
  `yoyakuhenkou` → `予約変更`, plus arrival, rescheduling and reception phrases.
- Aftercare and packaging: `i still have the receipt`, `bao'zhuang` → `包装`,
  `购买凭证怎么提供`, `hoshousho` → `保証書` and `hensouhouhou` → `返送方法`.
  `henkin` gains the independent common Word `返金`, without a new authored
  context of its own.

Related appointment, return, refund, receipt and repair vocabulary already
existed; some longer text was already composable as Sentence output. This
adds explicit Word entries and their own contexts, not the first ability to
type those characters or a new segmentation policy. The examples do not send
messages, book appointments, query live opening hours/queues, check actual
aftercare progress or determine store policies, warranty rights or refund
eligibility. No personal routines, contacts or input history are used.

Fallback remains independent of model providers: local candidates are available
while optional prediction is disabled, missing, pending, failed, timed out or
empty. Translation and open-ended prediction still depend on a configured
provider. No model is bundled or automatically downloaded.

## Measured local-input work

English stops rendering authored sentence remainders after its sixth **unique**
choice and reuses the current word's prefix/canonical form. Duplicates do not
consume that limit. Japanese uses an immutable startup-vocabulary membership
index for the same Word text, reading and Katakana forms; set iteration does
not supply candidate order. That index has a one-time owned-storage cost,
bounded to at most three forms per Word, not zero memory overhead.

Five alternating release-process replay pairs on the development machine
(Linux 7.0.0-38-generic / Ryzen 7735H) measured these warm engine mean-time
reductions: **English 33.77%**, **Japanese builtin 54.06%**, and **Japanese with
nine packs 57.66%**. The corresponding p95 reductions were **61.53%, 39.75%
and 50.14%**. These are medians of per-run timing summaries, not a universal
speedup, confidence interval or CI performance threshold. The replay tool
performs two untimed warmups followed by 30 corpus passes; five alternating
pairs are an outer measurement procedure. See the
[replay tool](../../examples/input_hotpath_bench.rs) and
[method and raw timing scope](../../DEVELOPMENT.md).

A Chinese offset-match cache experiment was **withdrawn**: its nominal mean
gain of 2.08% and p95 gain of 1.31% were too small to exclude timing noise or
justify extra temporary storage. Chinese production code was restored to the
pre-experiment bytes. The final Chinese control was actually slightly slower:
**mean elapsed time +3.70%, p95 +4.84%**. Run variation is possible, but neither
Chinese acceleration nor identical performance is claimed.

Both full JSONL outputs, covering **419 synthetic candidate vectors**, match
the saved pre-optimization release output byte-for-byte, including text,
labels, types, sources, score bits and order. This compares the same expanded
vocabulary before/after the retained optimizations, not every possible draft
or every old/new vocabulary ranking. Pack/index construction lies outside
warm timing: there is no measured cold-start, UI repaint, native-keyboard,
IBus, application, real-model latency or model-quality speedup claim.

## Compatibility and limits

Vocabulary enrichment can intentionally change choices and weight indices even
though ranking formulas are unchanged. The messaging batch adds `連絡漏れ` to
the second page of `renraku`; four older sentence weights decrease by 0.5 under
the existing mixed-index formula. The previous first-page text/order remains
protected, and the exact reviewed difference and initial failures are retained
in [development history](../../DEVELOPMENT.md), not hidden by a blanket golden
refresh. `包装` deliberately gains an exact Word rather than retaining its
former prefix-only pool unchanged.

Builtin independent **里/那 readings remain absent**. Full-pool competition,
including the private-pack long `na ben shu gei ni` branch, remains bounded by
the same six-slot pages and twelve-candidate list. Existing decoder beam,
completion and 256-character local/model budgets are not enlarged. Selection
and reseeding tests are engine workflows, not proof of physical-keyboard or
arbitrary native-application acceptance. See [known limitations](../known-limitations.md).

The model-independent prediction protocol and optional local/authorized-cloud
adapters retain their contracts. The data-only catalog remains **25 packs:
8 English, 8 Chinese and 9 Japanese**, with unchanged SDK/format and the same
**16 installed-pack limit**, including disabled packs. Pack changes require
host/panel restart; there is no executable-plugin system, hot reload or
automatic package download.

## Validation and publication gates

The earlier vocabulary and performance records document their own scoped
checks. The final versioned local run completes **2,313 Rust checks / zero failures /
32 explicit ignores**, across 213 unique result groups with zero filtered cases.
It includes all seven Japanese terminal-n cases, including the nine-pack matrix,
and all eight release-tail cases. Separately, the required **19/19 private UI gates**,
strict all-target/all-feature Clippy, formatting and source guards pass.
Ignored subprocess entry points are invoked by their parent tests and not counted twice;
the remaining opt-in native/data/settings/model checks are not accepted by this local run.
See the [final local record](../../DEVELOPMENT.md) for scope and retained logs.
No local result replaces the exact clean release commit's remote **linux,
linux-apps, linux-package, secrets, format, both Windows/macOS desktop checks
and linux-artifacts** gates. Every required gate must succeed.

Only that run's accepted Linux artifact may supply the two packages and their
SHA-256 files. Matching version/revision/clean-source manifests, complete
internal inventories, actual file hashes, and clean-container installation,
upgrade and removal must pass before tagging/publication. Old packages,
unaccepted staging and another commit's successful run are not substitutes.
The intended delivery is a **Linux Alpha prerelease, not latest stable**, with
Cargo version **0.9.2** and Android/macOS build **42**. This document does not
assert that those gates or publication have completed.

## Install or upgrade only after publication

Use assets from the [0.9.2 Release](https://github.com/chiharu-kiryu/Suzaku-map/releases/tag/v0.9.2)
only after they have passed acceptance and are actually published. Expected
assets are `suzaku_0.9.2_amd64.deb`,
`suzaku-0.9.2-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz`, and each file's `.sha256`.
Source preparation does not update an existing installation.

Finish/cancel the draft and fully quit Suzaku before upgrading:

```bash
sha256sum -c suzaku_0.9.2_amd64.deb.sha256
sudo apt install ./suzaku_0.9.2_amd64.deb
/usr/bin/suzaku-panel
```

For a first installation, register as the desktop user **without sudo** using
`/usr/bin/suzaku-tool linux-register install`, then
`/usr/bin/suzaku-tool linux-register verify`. Activation remains an explicit
tray action. Restart host and panel together after upgrading; existing settings
and pack registrations are retained. Archive users must verify the external
checksum and internal `SHA256SUMS` before running `./bin/panel`.
See [Linux packaging and data](../linux-packaging-data.md).
